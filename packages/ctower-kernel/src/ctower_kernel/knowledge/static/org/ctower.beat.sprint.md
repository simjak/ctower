# Sprint planning and reconciliation

Reconcile the recorded sprint state against verified reality rather than against the last report.
Pull the real counters for open change proposals and open work, confirm that no lane is idle and
that no ready change proposal is ageing, and refresh any board row that has gone stale. Drive the
gaps through the owning seat's inbox. Raise at most one consolidated operator question, and only
when a genuine decision blocks the sprint. Deliver the reconciliation as the Inbox work-item
artifact, counted in tasks rather than elapsed time.
