# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in ctower a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy
community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's sensitive information.
- Put the health of the project and community ahead of individual advantage.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized language or attention, trolling, sustained
disruption, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, issues, and other material that violates it, and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through a maintainer contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), with
**Code of Conduct** in the subject or first line. Do not disclose another person's sensitive information in
a public issue. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and avoid conflicts of interest when handling
a report.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on safety and repair. A
good-faith disagreement about design is not misconduct; harassment disguised as technical criticism is.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
